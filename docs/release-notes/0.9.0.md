# Bustle 0.9.0

Bustle gives a place a living population: people with needs, moods, schedules, work and relationships, run headless and cheaply, for any Godot 4.7 game. 0.9.0 is the living-population release. A set of new optional modules gives agents a working life, a private life and a life cycle. The demos show them all together. The simulation rings get two safeguards for large worlds.

Every new module is opt-in, and every new setting defaults to the 0.8.0 behaviour. Nothing is random: the same seed, inputs and clock replay the same run. APIs remain experimental before 1.0.

## What's new

**Work**
- **A job board** for posted, one-off work. Agents take the posting they can do and want most, through their brains. Claims are exclusive, and ties are settled without rolls.
- **Skills and passions:** XP from practice, levels from data, decay to a floor, and passions that speed learning. A skill in use feeds `work.speed` and `work.quality`.
- **Careers:** an agent's standing job held at a rank. Performance comes from attendance and the work itself, and reviews, promotion and demotion go through host hooks.

**Daily life**
- **Rooms and surroundings:** room scores (beauty, comfort, cleanliness, impressiveness) give environment thoughts, and indoors and outdoors move needs.
- **Wants and fears:** short-lived wants and event-formed fears, aspiration points and a reward hook for the host.
- **Romance:** a courtship ladder of courting, partner, engaged and married, with breakups, jealousy and family links. The host keeps the say over engagements and marriages.

**Life cycle and health**
- **Life stages:** ages on the clock (a long catch-up equals stepping day by day), data-defined stages that gate goals and options, and the host's birth and death hooks. A death releases the career, job claim and roles.
- **Health:** Bustle reacts to the host's health and keeps none of its own. Capacities slow work and movement, pain and illness bring thoughts, a downed agent picks no work, and an opt-in tend job has able agents ask the host to give care.

**Story**
- **A causal storyteller** that paces host-authored events by the story's tension. It has preconditions, cooldowns, the quest lock and a host veto, and it applies the thoughts, orders and spawn requests each event declares. There is no seed and no deck.

**Demos and docs**
- **Full demo sessions:** a 7-day house session and a 15-day colony session with every module. Each replays a pinned timeline with a clean stuck watch and still runs with each module left out in turn.
- **New guides:** a tutorial that builds a small demo from scratch, and a module guide.

**Simulation rings**
- **Bounded catch-up:** an agent that stays off-screen through a long clock jump is caught up at most seven game days (168 thinks). The older time is summarised, just as on leaving off-screen.
- **An optional FULL cap,** with a separate cap for interiors. Agents already FULL keep their places, the rest stay LIGHT, and free places go to the nearest waiting agent. It is off by default.

## Upgrading from 0.8.0

No public class, method, signal or setting was removed or renamed. Three changes can still affect a 0.8.0 host:

- **Long clock jumps:** an off-screen backlog longer than the catch-up cap is now summarised (`summary_hook` and `caught_up`) instead of replayed hour by hour.
- **`BustleSchedule.report()`** takes an optional `activity` argument. A subclass that overrides it must add the parameter.
- **Ports have new methods with safe defaults:** social kinds, the work port's board, room scores, life-stage gates, health readings and care, and spawn requests. A host subclass that already uses one of these names must rename its method.

The full list of changes is in `CHANGELOG.md`.
