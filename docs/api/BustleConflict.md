# BustleConflict

Generated by `tools/gen_api_docs.sh` from the `##` doc comments. Do not edit by hand.

**Experimental:** this API may change before 1.0.

Inherits: `RefCounted`

Fight, flee or fetch help, in the CRITICAL band. Nothing here is left to chance: every fight starts from a cause, and every intent to kill has a reason.
**Causes.** An agent that `notice()`s someone it regards as hostile (by the disposition port, never by opinion) is in a conflict. A `quarrel()` the host starts comes to blows only when its provocation and the agent's grudge reach a threshold set by temper and mood. A fetched ally fights for whoever fetched it.
**Stances.** Bold or hot-tempered agents fight; timid ones fetch a nearby ally, or flee. `fill_inputs()` writes the stance as the scorer's `threat.fight`, `threat.flee` and `threat.fetch_help` inputs, which the goals from `goal_defs()` read, and the `fight`, `flee` and `fetch_help` actions carry them out. When a conflict ends, its inputs go and the agent's usual goals (its schedule) win again.
**Intent.** A fighter means to `SUBDUE` unless it has a reason to `KILL`: `defend_life` (its threat means to kill it, and it is cornered or its life is threatened; checked again every fight tick, so a fight can escalate), or a reason the host gives through `lethal_reason_of`. `intent_changed` reports every change with its reason.
**Events.** Every fight that ends emits `assaulted`, and one in which the target died also `killed`, whatever the intent: a subdue fight can still kill. Each carries `{act, cause, cause_detail, intent, intent_reason, killed, at}`. `act` is a fixed id for the fight (attacker, victim and the minute it started); a host stores it on the wounds it causes, so a victim who dies later of them can be traced to the fight.

## Methods

### `notice`

```gdscript
func notice(agent: StringName, other: StringName, now: int, nearby: StringName[] = []) -> StringName
```

`agent` notices `other` at minute `now`. Only when it regards `other` as hostile does it take a stance, which it returns (`FIGHT`, `FLEE` or `FETCH_HELP`; a fetcher picks the nearest ally in `nearby`). Empty otherwise, or when it's already in a conflict.

### `quarrel`

```gdscript
func quarrel(a: StringName, b: StringName, now: int, provocation: float, nearby: StringName[] = []) -> bool
```

`a` quarrels with `b` at minute `now`, provoked by `provocation` (0 to 1, from the host: a harsh word, a shove). It comes to blows only when the provocation plus `grudge()` reaches `quarrel_threshold()`; then `a` fights and `b` takes its own stance. Nobody becomes hostile. True when it came to blows.

### `grudge`

```gdscript
func grudge(a: StringName, b: StringName) -> float
```

How much `a` holds against `b`, from 0 to 1: the sum of `a`'s negative opinion entries about `b` (most come from deeds) over `BustleConflictConfig.grudge_scale`.

### `quarrel_threshold`

```gdscript
func quarrel_threshold(agent: StringName) -> float
```

What a quarrel must reach for `agent` to come to blows: from the config's calm, middle and hot thresholds by its temper, lowered by a low mood.

### `stance_for`

```gdscript
func stance_for(agent: StringName, has_help: bool) -> StringName
```

The stance `agent` takes against a threat: `FIGHT` when the average of its boldness and temper reaches `BustleConflictConfig.fight_threshold`, else `FETCH_HELP` when `has_help`, else `FLEE`.

### `nearest_ally`

```gdscript
func nearest_ally(agent: StringName, nearby: StringName[], exclude: StringName = &"") -> StringName
```

The nearest ally of `agent` in `nearby` within `BustleConflictConfig.help_radius`, not in a conflict of its own, or empty. An ally regards `agent` as friendly, or shares its group without regarding it as hostile.

```gdscript

```

Writes `agent`'s stance into `inputs`: 1 for its stance and 0 for the others, or no `threat.*` stance inputs at all when it's in no conflict.

### `call_help`

```gdscript
func call_help(helper: StringName, agent: StringName, now: int) -> bool
```

`helper` answers `agent`'s call at minute `now`: it fights `agent`'s threat, whatever its own personality, and `agent` is out of the conflict. False when `agent` has no threat or `helper` is busy.

### `filter_damage`

```gdscript
func filter_damage(damage: float, current_health: float, intent: StringName) -> float
```

`damage` to a victim with `health`, as a fight with `intent` should deal it right away: unchanged for `KILL`, else cut so the victim keeps at least `BustleConflictConfig.scuffle_floor`. Never negative. A stopgap until a host's health system decides what blows do.

### `finish`

```gdscript

```

Ends `agent`'s fight at minute `now` from the combat port's `BustleCombatPort.outcome()`: emits `assaulted`, and `killed` when the target died, then clears the conflict and disengages.
