The numbers behind [BustleWitnessJudge](BustleWitnessJudge.md): what someone who knows of a theft does about it. Each outcome's score is `Σ weight × (value − 50) / 100` over its terms, plus any loyalty. Nothing here is a chance.

## Properties

### `weights`

```gdscript
var weights: Dictionary[StringName, Dictionary] = {...}
```

Outcome → term → weight. A term is a personality slider (`BustlePersonality.SLIDERS`) or one of `BustleWitnessJudge.TERMS`. By default every outcome weighs honesty, piety, boldness and diligence, the witness's opinions of the thief and the victim and its fear of the thief; reporting adds the authority's standing, confronting adds temper and gossiping adds sociability.

### `kind_weights`

```gdscript
var kind_weights: Dictionary[StringName, float] = {}
```

Relationship kind between the witness and the thief → loyalty, added to every outcome but silence (a negative loyalty, such as for kin, keeps a witness quiet). Empty by default.

### `threshold`

```gdscript
var threshold: float = 1.0
```

What the best outcome must reach; below it, the witness stays silent.
