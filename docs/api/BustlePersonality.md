# BustlePersonality

Generated by `tools/gen_api_docs.sh` from the `##` doc comments. Do not edit by hand.

**Experimental:** this API may change before 1.0.

Inherits: `RefCounted`

One agent's personality: 8 sliders every agent has, from 0 to 100 (50 is average), and up to `MAX_TRAITS` traits on top. Sliders reach the scorer as the inputs `trait.<slider>`, from 0 to 1. Traits are data ([BustleTraitDef](BustleTraitDef.md)); `apply()` puts them into effect: their modifiers into a [BustleModifierSet](BustleModifierSet.md) (one source per trait), their needs into a [BustleNeedSet](BustleNeedSet.md), their goals into a [BustleGoalLibrary](BustleGoalLibrary.md) and their permanent thoughts into a mood (anything with `set_held(def, held, details)`). Applying again after a change adds what is new and takes away what a removed trait brought, and nothing else.
