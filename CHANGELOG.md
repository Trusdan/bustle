# Changelog

All notable changes to Bustle. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Before 1.0, any minor version may change the API. Every public class is marked `@experimental` until then.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Ambition bias (see `docs/ambitions.md`): `BustleBrain.score_boost` (and `BustleScoreContext.score_boost`) multiplies each candidate's score last, never changing its band. `BustleAmbitions.boost` supplies it from each template's `boosts` table: off while locked, for a disabled template (even one already assigned), for `never_boost` goals (theft and fights) and toward anyone but a targeted template's target. `BustleAmbitionTargets` fills the courting (someone liked nearby, never family) and feud (the rival) inputs, with the courting target switching to someone else before the ambition is moot. A saved ambition whose template is gone now ends with `ended_moot`.
- Ambitions (see `docs/ambitions.md`), assigning and ending only. `BustleAmbitions` gives an agent one long-term aim from `BustleAmbitionDef` templates (save money, get promoted, court someone, settle a feud, leave): the eligible template with the highest slider score, ties by id, a host pin first, never a disabled template, with no chance. An ambition completes when its progress input or fact says so (the `achieved_ambition` deed and a 7-day cooldown for that template), ends as moot when it can no longer happen (no cooldown), and is never given up. The developer setting `bustle/simulation/ambitions` defaults to on. Ambitions save and load.
- Mental state behaviour (see `docs/mood.md`): `BustleMentalBehaviour` sets each state's modifiers and gives goals in the guarded band (`BustleMentalAction`): withdraw goes home and turns ordinary conversation away (new `BustleConversation.accepts`, `BustleMentalBehaviour.accepts`), wander walks about, binge uses a known indulgence, and lash out insults the most disliked person nearby (same area, within a short walk, rivals first), once per person an episode, with any fight through the usual quarrel rules. Locks (`is_locked`) hold states, goals and modifiers off. `BustleComfort.low_state_of` lets a friend in a low state be comforted even as its mood rises.
- Mental states (see `docs/mood.md`), deciding and recording only. `BustleMentalState` starts a state the moment mood crosses a personality-adjusted line: withdraw, sulk, wander, binge or lash out below the break threshold (highest slider score, quieter first on ties, binge and lash out only with an indulgence or someone disliked nearby), or high spirits or inspiration above their lines (inspiration wins). States end on time or when mood recovers, and a low one leaves a stacking catharsis thought. There's no chance, no timer between episodes and no limit shared between agents. `BustleMentalStateDef.Behaviour` lists everything a state can be. The developer setting `bustle/simulation/mental_states` defaults to on. States save and load.
- Report errands (see `docs/crime.md`): reporting, telling the victim and gossiping now count only when the agent reaches whoever it's for, face to face. A `deliver` goal in the guarded band runs `BustleDeliverAction`, which names where to go and asks for the `hurry` gear (new `BustleIntent.gear`). An errand waits while nobody holds the role or `can_travel` is false. It's dropped when its memory fades or under `intimidate`, though the memory stays and `reconsider` decides again. Whoever is told decides in turn. Reports carry a `source`: `eyewitness`, `secondhand` or `owner_deed`. Instant delivery is now a developer and test option, off by default (`bustle/simulation/instant_crime_reports`).
- Witness decisions (see `docs/crime.md`): everyone who knows of a theft (a witness, or an owner who finds a deed) remembers it and decides one thing to do: report, tell the victim, confront the thief (words only), gossip or stay silent. `BustleWitnessJudge` decides from personality, opinions of the thief and the victim, fear of the thief, the authority's standing and loyalty, with weights in `BustleWitnessConfig` and no chance; a host can replace it with `BustleCrimeWatch.should_report`. New `decided`, `confronted`, `victim_told` and `gossiped` events, and `BustleCrimeWatch.question` for what an agent can still tell. Reports are still made at once.
- `BustleCrimeWatch` (see `docs/crime.md`): what becomes of each theft. Only agents that perceived it are witnesses (the host's `perceives`, by default anyone there who isn't incapacitated); each remembers it and reports it to an authority role (`crime_reported`, with the role's holder from the roles port). A theft nobody perceived is a deed: its owner finds it later (`notice_losses`, `deed_found`) and reports it without knowing the thief, and `stolen_goods_of` lists the goods that point to it. Records save and load. `BustlePropertyPort.remove` lets the host use up an item. `BustleCrime`'s default judgement of being seen now ignores incapacitated agents.
- Crime (`addons/bustle/core/crime/`, see `docs/crime.md`): `BustleCrime`, desperate theft with no chance and no cap. Guards run in order: the `bustle/simulation/npc_crime` developer setting and the host's switch; the agent can act; a pressing need; that pressure plus personality (honesty holds back, greed and boldness push on) reaching a threshold; and an unseen, unprotected item of someone else's that eases the need. A `steal` goal in the new `BustleBands.GUARDED` band runs the `steal` action (`BustleStealAction`), which checks the guards again, moves the item and emits a neutral `theft` event with what drove it. Also `BustleCrimeConfig`, and a new property port, `BustlePropertyPort`: an in-memory register of owners, places, relief and protected items, with transfer-only moves and export and import.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-09

Cause-driven conflict: fight, flee or fetch help, an intent to subdue or kill only for a reason, non-lethal scuffles that leave at least 1 health, quarrels from provocation and grudge against a temper and mood threshold, and assaulted and killed events that carry the act id, the cause and the intent. Nothing in the conflict code is left to chance. The combat port takes an intent and reports how a fight ended, and the health port reports when a life is threatened. APIs remain experimental before 1.0.

### Added

- Conflict (`addons/bustle/core/conflict/`, see `docs/conflict.md`), with nothing left to chance: `BustleConflict` (fights start from a cause: hostility through the disposition port, a quarrel whose provocation and grudge pass a threshold set by temper and mood, or a call for help; fight, flee or fetch help from boldness and temper; `threat.*` inputs and three CRITICAL goals; an intent to subdue or kill, killing only for a reason (defending one's life, or one the host gives), checked again every fight tick with `intent_changed`; `filter_damage` for a subdue fight's immediate damage; `assaulted` and `killed` events with a fixed act id, the cause, the intent and its reason; export and import), `BustleConflictConfig`, and the `fight`, `flee` and `fetch_help` actions. `BustleCombatPort.engage` takes an intent, and `outcome` reports how a fight ended. `BustleHealthPort.is_life_threatened`.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-09

Needs, mood and thoughts, affordances, schedules with roles, jobs and wages, personality sliders and trait bundles, memory and FindPerson, social opinions, greetings, conversations and comfort, and buying through the trade port, with owners taking from their own stock for free. APIs remain experimental before 1.0.

### Added

- Buying (see `docs/affordances.md`): `BustleTradePort` gains `price_of`, `can_buy` (what `buy` checks; a host keeping stock or lending credit overrides the two together), `can_take` and `take` (an owner using its own stock without paying) and `export_state`/`import_state` for the default's balances. `BustleAffordanceDef.item`: each use buys that item from the owner or payee through the trade port, or the owner takes one from its own stock without paying, and is offered only while that would go through. A neutral demo food stall in `demo/trade/`.
- Conversations and comfort (see `docs/social.md`): `BustleConversation` (turns that pick interactions by weight from sliders, `conversation.<id>.weight` modifiers, opinion and a low mood's pull towards curt ones; a low mood lowers the chance to start; `run` all at once or the `converse` action, `BustleConverseAction`, one turn per tick, with the same results; an optional opener) and `BustleComfort` (friends who notice an agent in a low mood are offered comforting it, weighted by `need.comfort.weight`). `BustleInteractionDef` gains `weight` and `slider_weights`. A neutral demo interaction set and comfort goal in `demo/social/`.
- Interactions and greeting (see `docs/social.md`): `BustleInteractionDef` (opinion values for each side, a seeded spread, how much compatibility and mood bend them, duration, a thought for each side, tags) and `BustleSocialInteractions` (one pure `outcome` function; `interact` records a deed and the opinion entries that name it, plus thoughts; `greet` with per-pair and per-greeter cooldowns, saved). `BustleSocialStore` gains `deeds_of` and `opinions_of`, and the inspector's snapshot shows an agent's deeds and opinion reasons under `social`.
- The built-in social store (`addons/bustle/society/`, see `docs/social.md`): `BustleSocialStore`, a `BustleSocialPort` keeping one-way opinions (50 plus live entries, each with a plain-data reason and a duration in game days, clamped to 0 to 100), host-set relationship kinds and kinds formed from repeated deeds by `BustleRelationshipRules` (friend and rival with forming and release levels, a deed count and a window; `kind_changed`), the port's deed behaviour, and atomic export and import. `BustleCompatibility.of` gives each pair a fixed value from -1 to 1 per world seed, optionally blended with how close their personality sliders are.
- Memory and FindPerson (`addons/bustle/core/memory/`, see `docs/memory.md`): `BustleMemoryConfig` (capacity 32, a half-life per kind, a starting strength per source, forgetting below 0.1), `BustleMemory` (people, places and items with an area, an optional position and a source of saw, heard, told or routine; strength halving per half-life; a weaker note never overwriting a stronger one; the weakest dropped past capacity; export and import) and `BustleFindPerson` (sense, then memory, then a routine guess, then asking, then giving up with a cooldown). `BustleMemoryFeed` in the perception module writes memory from `perception_changed`.
- Personality (`addons/bustle/core/personality/`, see `docs/personality.md`): `BustlePersonality` (8 sliders from 0 to 100, given to the scorer as `trait.<slider>` from 0 to 1; up to 3 traits; `apply` puts trait modifiers, needs, goals and permanent thoughts into effect and takes away exactly what a removed trait brought; export and import) and `BustleTraitDef` (a data-only bundle of modifier adds and multipliers, slider shifts, added needs and goals, permanent thoughts and exclusions). Five neutral demo traits in `demo/traits/` (loner, outgoing, low-spirited, greedy, pious).
- Jobs as roles and wages (see `docs/schedules.md`): `BustleRoleSchedules` (each role's routine and employer) and `BustleScheduleRoster` (every agent's schedule by job, swapped at once when a role's holder changes, with `settle` paying wages). Schedule blocks gain `wage` and `payer`; `BustleSchedule` gains `set_def` (keeping attendance and erasing the old routine's inputs) and `settle` (each attended block's wage paid once after it ends through the trade port, a missed block paying nothing and an unpaid wage staying owed). `BustleWorkPort`'s default can read jobs from a roster. Blocks can't use the activity `free_time` or a negative wage, and `BustleScheduleDef.is_valid` requires a week of at least one day.
- Schedules (`addons/bustle/core/schedules/`, see `docs/schedules.md`): `BustleScheduleBlock` (an activity from one minute of the day to another, across midnight if needed, at a place, with a lateness tolerance), `BustleScheduleDay`, `BustleScheduleDef` (the usual day with weekday and season overrides, a weekday winning over a season) and `BustleSchedule` (the current block, the scorer's `schedule.<activity>` and `schedule.free_time` inputs, attendance as on time, late, missed or pending, export and import). `BustleClockPort.season()` (empty by default).
- Affordances (`addons/bustle/core/affordances/`, see `docs/affordances.md`): `BustleAffordanceDef` (relief, side effects, tags, capacity, hold time, price, how welcome non-owners are), `BustleAffordance` (a placed object: reservations that lapse and renew, capacity, ownership with an access hook, a price paid through the trade port with a cost hook, advertisements for the scorer, and `use` that pays, applies need effects with leisure tolerance and releases, changing nothing on refusal) and `BustleAffordanceSet` (a population's affordances, advertised in id order, optionally only those an agent knows of). Owners, payees and holds export and import.
- The `needs_mood` module, part 1 (`addons/bustle/modules/needs_mood/`, see `docs/mood.md`): `BustleThoughtDef` (value, duration or held while a condition holds, stack limit and multiplier, emotion, tags, reason kind, optional need threshold), `BustleMoodConfig` (baseline 50, rise 12 and fall 8 per game hour, volatility, range 0 to 100) and `BustleMood` (target = baseline + thoughts with stacking and `thought.tag.<tag>` modifiers; closed-form approach that splits only at expiries; frozen while asleep; dominant emotion with ties keeping the current one; every thought's reason; need thoughts from a `BustleNeedSet`; the scorer's `mood` input; export and import).
- The needs engine (`addons/bustle/core/needs/`, see `docs/needs.md`): `BustleNeedDef` (a need as data: base rate per game hour, the conditions it rises under, per-condition changes, response curve, start) and `BustleNeedSet` (one agent's pressures from 0 to 1, advanced linearly by game minutes under host-named conditions, the `need.<id>.rate` modifier scaling the rise only, relief and side effects, `need.<id>` inputs and curves for the scorer, export and import). The social pair through `social_conditions` (alone, in company, crowded) and the `social.crowd_tolerance` modifier (base 6). `BustleLeisure`: leisure kinds with per-kind tolerance that builds with use, decays per game day and, at the boredom threshold, removes a kind's relief so the scorer picks another.

### Fixed

- Perception awareness no longer leaks: each detection meter's signal is connected to a bound method instead of a lambda that held the awareness, so awareness and its meters are freed.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-08

The headless simulation core: the scorer, brain, ports, society registry, orders, debug tools and simulation rings. The perception module is unchanged.

### Added

- The utility scorer's headless core (`addons/bustle/core/brain/`):
  - `BustleBands`: priority bands as spaced whole numbers; only the highest band with an eligible candidate is chosen from.
  - `BustleInputs`: the fixed input families (`need`, `trait`, `opinion`, `memory`, `distance`, `schedule`, `role`, `fact`, `time`, `health`, `threat`) and `is_valid_input`.
  - `BustleConsideration`, `BustleGoalDef` (band, base weight, `min_score`, `min_commit_minutes`, preconditions, considerations, `uses_advertisements`) and `BustleGoalLibrary` (with `problems()`).
  - `BustleAdvertisement`, `BustleScoringConfig` and `BustleScoreContext`.
  - `BustleUtilityScorer`: goal scores, the signed advertisement score, eligibility with reasons, stable ordering, the 10%-of-best window and a uniform seeded pick, with an IDLE fallback; `BustleCandidate` holds each result.
- `BustleChoiceKeeper`: hysteresis around the scorer. The current choice gets ×1.15 momentum on its selection score (eligibility still uses the raw score), is kept while inside the pick window and through its goal's `min_commit_minutes`, and is interrupted at once by a strictly higher band. Failed goals cool down (`BustleGoalDef.fail_cooldown_minutes`, and at least the minute they failed). Its state exports and imports as plain data.
- `BustleModifierSet`: keyed numbers that sources add to and multiply, applied in sorted source order, with `fill_context` for the scorer's need weights and tag multipliers.
- `BustleGoalLibrary.find` and `BustleGoalDef.fail_cooldown_minutes`.
- Action sequences (`addons/bustle/core/brain/actions/`): `BustleActionStep` (action, params, on fail RETRY/SKIP/ABORT, `max_retries` after the first attempt), `BustleActionSequence`, `BustleActionContext`, `BustleActionHandler` and `BustleSequenceRunner`. The runner does at most one handler tick and one start per tick, cleans up an unfinished attempt exactly once, interrupts and resumes (restarting the step), and saves and restores a step mid-way. Built-in `wait` and `idle` actions run for game minutes.
- `BustleBrain`, `BustleSenses` and `BustleIntent`: a headless brain whose `tick(delta, senses)` scores, chooses with hysteresis, runs the chosen goal's sequence (`BustleGoalDef.sequence`, with the chosen option on `BustleActionContext.option_id`) and returns a typed intent with its reasons and the step's output. A failed sequence cools its goal down. The generator is seeded once from a fixed 64-bit FNV-1a of the world seed and agent id (`seed_for`), and `export_state`/`import_state` save the generator, keeper, runner and running goal so a run continues identically; a state that doesn't fit is refused without half-restoring.
- The ports, each with a standalone default so Bustle runs with no host: `BustleSocialPort` (neutral opinions; deeds known first-hand by the actor and named witnesses, shared one hop), `BustleRolesPort` with `BustleRoleTable`, `BustleWorldPort` (one area, no bodies), `BustleClockPort` (its own minutes, 1440 a day), `BustleCombatPort` (resolves harmlessly), `BustleTradePort` (in-memory balances that never create or lose money), `BustleWorkPort`, `BustleSkillSet`, `BustleLifeStagePort`, `BustleHealthPort`, `BustleEnvironmentPort` and `BustleDirectorPort`. `BustlePorts` holds one of each, `BustleDispositionPort` included (unchanged).
- `BustleSociety`, `BustleNpcState` and `BustleImportReport` (`addons/bustle/society/`): the scene-independent registry of a population. Agents are added from registered archetypes (a goal library and scoring config), each with a brain, an area, a position and module data; bodies attach and detach without touching the state. `export_state` gives plain data with a schema version; `import_state` checks everything first and replaces the population all at once, returning a report and changing nothing when a payload is malformed, another schema version, or doesn't fit. 64-bit numbers (the world seed, brains' seeds and generator states) are exported as decimal strings so they survive JSON exactly, and import accepts strings or ints. `state_hash` is a SHA-256 of the canonical export, with whole-number floats written as ints so a JSON round trip hashes the same. `set_port` replaces a port with one of the right kind.
- Orders and autonomy: `BustleOrder` and a per-agent `BustleOrderQueue` (first in, first out; submissions refused as impossible with a reason; DONE, FAILED with the step's reason, CANCELLED, reported once by signal and on the intent). Orders sit in the new `BustleBands.ORDERED` (70, below URGENT), forced ones in `BustleBands.FORCED` (110, above CRITICAL). While an order runs the self-chosen goal is interrupted, and it resumes afterwards when it is still eligible and in the top band. `BustleBrain.autonomy` (OFF: orders only; LOW: URGENT and CRITICAL only; NORMAL and FULL: the whole scorer) and `set_player_controlled` (the brain never chooses; orders are cancelled and refused) are saved with the brain; orders aren't. `BustleSociety.order` and `cancel_order`. `BustleSequenceRunner.has_handler` and `share_handlers_with`.
- Debug tools (`addons/bustle/debug/`), for debug builds only (`BustleDebug.is_available()`): `BustleInspector` reads an agent's goal, band, step, top 3 scores (the scorer's own candidates), order queue and need inputs, and marks rings, memory, schedules and the needs module unavailable; `BustleDebugConsole` runs `help`, `agents`, `why`, `need` and `order`; `BustleDebugOverlay` labels agents' bodies and stops processing when off. `BustleBrain` keeps its last senses, candidates and intent for inspection, and `BustleOrderQueue.peek` reads a queued order.
- Simulation rings, part 1 (`addons/bustle/core/rings/`): `BustleSimRingConfig` and `BustleSimRings` sort a society's agents into FULL, LIGHT and OFF_SCREEN rings from the world and clock ports, areas first and metres second: FULL in the player's area within `full_radius` (60 m), or anywhere in it when it is an interior; LIGHT farther, without a position, or one area away; OFF_SCREEN otherwise. Changes by distance keep a 15 m hysteresis band and a 5 s dwell; changes by area apply at once (FULL to OFF_SCREEN included), except LIGHT to OFF_SCREEN, which waits until the area has been out of reach for 10 s. `ring_changed`, `counts` and each ring's entry minute. Classifying never touches an agent's state. `BustleWorldPort.player_position()` and `is_interior()`.
- Simulation rings, part 2a: `BustleSimRings` spawns a body through the world port when an agent becomes FULL, at its last position, or (when that is within `no_pop_in_distance`, 20 m, of the player and in view) at the nearest spot out of view within that distance, retrying later when there is none; and keeps the position and frees the body when it stops being FULL (`manage_bodies`, `body_spawned`, `body_freed`). `BustleNpcState.sim_ring` and `last_update_minute` are saved by the society (optional on import), and a restored ring is kept, so an agent in the hysteresis band stays FULL after a load. `BustleWorldPort.is_in_view()`.
- Simulation rings, part 2b: `BustleSimRings.tick(delta)` lets each agent think as its ring allows (FULL at `full_brain_hz` 2, LIGHT at `light_rate_hz` 1, OFF_SCREEN once per `offscreen_interval_game_minutes` 60 of game time), at fixed per-agent phases, through a `senses_provider`, setting `last_update_minute`; `catch_up(id)` runs hourly thinking from `last_update_minute`, at most `catch_up_cap_game_days` (7), summarising older time through `summary_hook`, and runs when an agent leaves OFF_SCREEN (`caught_up`); `last_timings` per ring. `bench/rings_bench.gd` and a report-only CI step.
